Predict, investigate, explain

  1. Count & graph: choose the coffee reports. Count students reporting at least one cup. Compare exact values with histogram bins. What changes when the bins change?
  2. Center & spread: choose the message counts. Predict the effect of a sixth friend with 500 messages. Include that friend, then explain the changes using the sorted values and squared deviations.
  3. Collect samples: collect ten samples from all 24 students. Keep the population fixed and compare their means. Start again using only the near-campus students. Distinguish sampling variation from a selection problem.

The interactive investigations require JavaScript. The reading has static graphs and worked explanations.
