  1. Use the probability-table view to identify an event’s bars, build its expected value, and compare the model with simulated observations.
  2. In the trial-sequence view, choose a teaching setting and generate several sequences. Read each row twice: count all successes, then locate the first success.
  3. Inspect the final geometric bar. It retains the probability of waiting beyond the displayed trials. Both observed distributions use every generated sequence and share their graphs’ probability scale with the theoretical model.

In the skywatch preset, a success is a window containing at least one qualified event. Multiple lights in one window still contribute one success. The geometric question imagines continuing the same protocol until the first positive window.
