Geometry of Categorical Models

A categorical model is a point in a shape. With 2 categories the shape is a line segment, with 3 categories it is a triangle, and with 4 categories it is a tetrahedron. This demo shows the expected model point, the observed data point, and their weighted squared departure, measured by chi-squared.

1. Choose the model size

The total sample size is fixed at 60. That is why one count is always dependent on the others. Sliders and “Explore” choose possible allocations; “Draw under H₀” below samples from the expected model. Rounding expected counts gives a whole-number approximation and may leave χ² above zero.

2. What the picture means

Chi-squared: χ2 = Σ (O - E)2 / E
Degrees of freedom: df = k - 1

The expected counts define a target point in the shape. The observed counts define another point. The chi-squared statistic is a weighted squared departure: χ² = N Σ(p̂ − p)²/p. Its value is not ordinary screen distance between the points.

5. Standardized components

These are the coordinates of the standardized difference vector \(z=(O-E)/\sqrt{E}\). The chi-squared statistic is the sum of their squares.

Tip: in the 4-category case, drag inside the picture to rotate the tetrahedron.

6. Turn the mismatch into a right-tail probability

Squaring the residuals combines departures in either direction into a nonnegative statistic. The p-value is the area to the right of the observed χ². Pale shading marks the rejection region at the chosen α; dark shading is the current p-value.

7. Let repeated tests under H₀ pile up

Each draw generates 60 independent categorical observations using the fixed expected probabilities. The latest sample moves the observed point above and adds a row below. Red points reject this true null model; the dashed cutoff is fixed by df and α.

Compare the rejection fraction with nominal α. Counts are discrete and the chi-squared reference distribution is an approximation, so the long-run fraction need not equal α exactly. This rate is not the probability that a particular null claim is true. Choose α before analyzing real data; changing it here compares thresholds on the same draws without changing their statistics or p-values.

Changing the expected model, category labels, or number of categories clears the sampled run. Moving the observed sliders only explores the current table; it does not add simulated tests.
