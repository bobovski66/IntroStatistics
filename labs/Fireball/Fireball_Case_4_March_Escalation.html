An independent statistical review has been requested after the American Meteor Society reported an unusual concentration of high-witness fireball events in the first quarter of 2026.

Question under review

Did the 2026 pattern change in March, or does the 3,229-report event create the appearance of a monthly surge?

Evidence used in this file

Current assignment

Compare monthly centers and threshold proportions, remove the largest event temporarily, and determine whether March remains different.

Investigation log

The threshold case found elevated Q1 counts at the upper witness tiers. The current file asks when within the quarter that pattern becomes strongest.

Statistical boundary: Witness-report counts are properties of the reporting system as well as the event. They can be affected by brightness and physical scale, but also by population, visibility, weather, geography, media attention, and reporting behavior. Claims about physical causes require evidence beyond this CSV.
