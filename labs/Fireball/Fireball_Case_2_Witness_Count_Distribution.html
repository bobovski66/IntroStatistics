Question under review

Investigation log

Case File 1 established that reports_count is a witness-volume variable within a selected 25+ event subset. It is not a direct measurement of object mass or brightness.

Statistical boundary: Witness-report counts are properties of the reporting system as well as the event. They can be affected by brightness and physical scale, but also by population, visibility, weather, geography, media attention, and reporting behavior. Claims about physical causes require evidence beyond this CSV.

Part 3. Remove one event temporarily

The March 8 western-Europe event has 3,229 witness reports. Removing it is not automatically justified, but a sensitivity calculation shows how much influence it has.

Interpretation: A data point may be genuine and still be influential. “Outlier” describes its position in a distribution; it does not mean the event is erroneous.

Part 4. Use resistant summaries

A 10% trimmed mean removes the lowest and highest 10% of values before averaging. It uses more information than the median while reducing the influence of the tails.
